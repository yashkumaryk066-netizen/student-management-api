Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: February 12, 2026

Strict No-Refund Policy: At Y.S.M AI, we are committed to providing high-quality software. We strongly encourage all potential customers to take a Live Demo and ask all necessary questions before making a purchase. Once a subscription is paid, no refunds will be issued under any circumstances.

1. Why We Have a No-Refund Policy

Our software is provided as a Service (SaaS) on a monthly or annual subscription basis. Because we offer a Free Live Demo and comprehensive product tours, we believe customers have sufficient opportunity to evaluate the software before committing.

Key Points:

  • ✅ Free Demo: Always take a demo to ensure the product meets your needs.
  • ✅ Monthly Billing: Since we offer monthly plans, you are not locked into long-term commitments. If you are not satisfied, you can simply choose not to renew for the next month.
  • ✅ Immediate Access: As soon as you pay, server resources and AI licenses are allocated to your account, which cannot be recovered.

2. Cancellation Process

You can cancel your subscription at any time. When you cancel:

  • Your access to the platform will continue until the end of your current paid billing period.
  • No further charges will be made to your account.
  • No refund will be provided for the remaining days of the current month/period.

3. Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds will NOT be provided in any case, including but not limited to:

  • ❌ Change of mind after purchase.
  • ❌ "I didn't use the software this month."
  • ❌ Accidental purchase or renewal.
  • ❌ Feature requests that are not currently in the software.
  • ❌ Technical issues on the client's side (e.g., poor internet, old hardware).

4. Exceptional Cases

In the extremely rare event of a duplicate charge or a technical billing error from our side, we will investigate and process a correction. In such cases, contact us within 24 hours of the transaction.

5. Refund Processing Timeline

  • Bank Transfer/UPI: 3-5 business days
  • Credit/Debit Card: 7-10 business days
  • Online Wallets: 5-7 business days
  • International Payments: 10-14 business days

Note: Processing time may vary depending on your bank or payment provider. We initiate refunds within 48 hours of approval.

Option 1: Self-Service (Instant)

  2. Go to Settings → Subscription
  3. Click "Cancel Subscription"
  4. Confirm cancellation and select reason
  5. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period

Upon cancellation, you have 30 days to export your data:

8. Upgrade/Downgrade Policy

Upgrading Your Plan:

  • Pay only the pro-rated difference for the remaining period
  • Instant access to upgraded features
  • No processing fees for upgrades

Downgrading Your Plan:

  • Downgrade takes effect at next renewal cycle
  • No refund for the current period
  • You retain access to premium features until renewal date

9. Exceptional Circumstances

In certain cases, we may offer refunds outside the standard policy:

  • Extended service outages (>72 hours)
  • Critical feature unavailability affecting core functionality
  • Billing errors or duplicate charges
  • Force majeure events (natural disasters, pandemics)

These are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. Contact support for review.

10. Chargeback Policy

⚠️ Please Contact Us Before Filing a Chargeback

If you file a chargeback without contacting us first:

We're here to help resolve any payment issues amicably. Please reach out to us first!
