Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: February 12, 2026

We want you to be completely satisfied with Y.S.M AI. Our refund policy is designed to be fair and transparent. Please read the terms below carefully.

1. 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We offer a full refund with no questions asked if you cancel within 30 days of your initial purchase.

Eligibility:

  • ✅ Valid for new subscribers only (first-time purchase)
  • ✅ Must request refund within 30 calendar days of payment
  • ✅ 100% refund to original payment method
  • ✅ No deductions or processing fees

2. Refunds After 30 Days

If you cancel after the 30-day guarantee period, refunds are calculated on a pro-rated basis:

Pro-Rated Refund Formula:

Refund Amount = (Annual Fee ÷ 12) × Unused Months - Processing Fee (₹200)

Example Calculations:

  • Scenario 1: School Plan (₹4,999), cancelled after 3 months
    → Refund = (₹4,999 ÷ 12) × 9 months - ₹200 = ₹3,549
  • Scenario 2: Institute Plan (₹6,999), cancelled after 6 months
    → Refund = (₹6,999 ÷ 12) × 6 months - ₹200 = ₹3,300
  • Scenario 3: Coaching Plan (₹2,999), cancelled after 10 months
    → Refund = (₹2,999 ÷ 12) × 2 months - ₹200 = ₹300

⚠️ Important Notes:

  • Refunds are available only if at least 2 months remain in your subscription
  • No refunds for subscriptions cancelled in the last month
  • Processing fee of ₹200 applies to all pro-rated refunds

3. Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds will NOT be provided in the following cases:

  • ❌ Account terminated due to Terms of Service violation
  • ❌ Fraudulent payment or chargeback disputes
  • ❌ Account suspended for non-payment
  • ❌ Subscription already expired or renewed
  • ❌ Custom development or integration services (if purchased separately)
  • ❌ Training or consulting services rendered

4. How to Request a Refund

Follow these steps to initiate a refund:

  1. Submit Request:
    Email support@ysm.education with subject line "Refund Request - [Your Institution Name]"
  2. Provide Details:
    Include: Transaction ID/UTR, payment date, registered email, and reason for cancellation
  3. Verification:
    Our team will verify your account and payment details (1-2 business days)
  4. Approval:
    You'll receive a refund approval email with amount breakdown
  5. Processing:
    Refund will be processed to your original payment method within 7-14 business days

5. Refund Processing Timeline

  • Bank Transfer/UPI: 3-5 business days
  • Credit/Debit Card: 7-10 business days
  • Online Wallets: 5-7 business days
  • International Payments: 10-14 business days

Note: Processing time may vary depending on your bank or payment provider. We initiate refunds within 48 hours of approval.

6. Cancellation Process

You can cancel your subscription anytime through:

Option 1: Self-Service (Instant)

  1. Login to your Y.S.M AI dashboard
  2. Go to Settings → Subscription
  3. Click "Cancel Subscription"
  4. Confirm cancellation and select reason
  5. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period

Option 2: Contact Support

  • 📧 Email: support@ysm.education
  • 📱 WhatsApp: +91 8356926231

7. Data Export After Cancellation

Upon cancellation, you have 30 days to export your data:

Available Data Export Formats:

  • 📊 Student Records (CSV, Excel)
  • 📈 Attendance Reports (PDF, CSV)
  • 💰 Payment History (CSV, Excel)
  • 📚 Academic Results (CSV, PDF)
  • 📄 Uploaded Documents (ZIP)

⚠️ Important: After 30 days, your data will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

8. Upgrade/Downgrade Policy

Upgrading Your Plan:

  • Pay only the pro-rated difference for the remaining period
  • Instant access to upgraded features
  • No processing fees for upgrades

Downgrading Your Plan:

  • Downgrade takes effect at next renewal cycle
  • No refund for the current period
  • You retain access to premium features until renewal date

9. Exceptional Circumstances

In certain cases, we may offer refunds outside the standard policy:

  • Extended service outages (>72 hours)
  • Critical feature unavailability affecting core functionality
  • Billing errors or duplicate charges
  • Force majeure events (natural disasters, pandemics)

These are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. Contact support for review.

10. Chargeback Policy

⚠️ Please Contact Us Before Filing a Chargeback

If you file a chargeback without contacting us first:

  • Your account will be immediately suspended
  • You forfeit the right to refund or data export
  • We may pursue legal action for fraudulent chargebacks

We're here to help resolve any payment issues amicably. Please reach out to us first!

11. Disputes and Resolution

  2. Provide your case reference number and detailed explanation
  3. A senior team member will review within 3-5 business days
  4. Final decision will be communicated via email
