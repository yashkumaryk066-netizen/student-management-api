Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 4, 2026

Your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy explains how Y.S.M AI Education System collects, uses, and protects your data. We are committed to ensuring that your institutional and student data remains private and secure.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our services:

1.1 Personal & Institutional Information

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, institution name
  • Student Data: Student names, classes, attendance records, exam results (managed by you)
  • Payment Information: Transaction IDs and UTRs (Processed via secure gateways; we do not store full card details)
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, and login timestamps for security audits

2. AI Data Privacy (Important)

Strict AI Privacy Commitment:

  • ❌ No Training: We do NOT use your institutional or student data to train our AI models or third-party models (OpenAI, Gemini, Claude).
  • 🔒 Isolated Processing: AI queries are processed in isolated sessions and are not stored in a way that can be linked back to individual students for model training purposes.
  • 👤 Anonymization: When interacting with AI modules, personal identifiers are minimized to ensure maximum privacy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide, maintain, and improve our education management platform.
  • Communication: To send important updates, system notifications, and support messages.
  • Security: To protect against unauthorized access and maintain system integrity.
  • No Selling: We NEVER sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Security Measures:

  • 🔒 Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS and at rest using AES-256.
  • 🛡️ Authentication: Secure JWT token-based authentication for all API sessions.
  • 🔐 Access Control: Strict role-based permissions ensure only authorized staff can see sensitive data.
  • 📊 Monitoring: 24/7 security monitoring and automated audit logging.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT share your data with anyone except:

  • Service Providers: Trusted partners (e.g., cloud hosting) who help us operate. They are legally bound to protect your data.
  • Legal Requirements: Only if required by a valid legal order or government authority.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as your subscription is active. Upon cancellation:

  • You have 30 days to export your institutional data.
  • After 30 days, your data is permanently purged from our primary servers.
